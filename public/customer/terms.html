Spin the Wheel — Terms & Conditions

  1. The promotion ("Spin the Wheel") runs from 1 to 31 December 2026 at participating MultiChoice Botswana outlets.
  2. Entry is limited to customers whose smartcard appears on the promotion whitelist prepared by the CVM team. Appearing on the whitelist does not guarantee a specific prize.
  3. One spin per smartcard for the duration of the promotion. A smartcard that has already spun cannot spin again, even if it is re-submitted in a later whitelist upload.
  4. Entrants must be 18 years or older and must accept these terms before spinning.
  5. Every spin wins a prize. The prize is determined at the moment of spinning by a random draw at the published odds, performed by the system; the wheel animation illustrates, but does not determine, the result.
  6. Prize tiers and odds, correct as published at launch and locked for the duration of the promotion: Airtime/data top-up (BWP 20) at 70%; Shopping voucher (BWP 50) at 25%; Shopping voucher (BWP 100) at 3%; Shopping voucher (BWP 150) at 1%; Grand prize voucher (BWP 2,000) at 1%.
  7. Prizes are fulfilled manually by the MultiChoice Botswana CVM team and are not credited automatically. MultiChoice Botswana will contact winners on the mobile number provided to arrange delivery.
  8. A prize that cannot be delivered after three recorded attempts (for example, an unreachable number) may be closed as unclaimed.
  9. MultiChoice Botswana collects only the smartcard number, mobile number and the consents given on this page for the purposes of this promotion, and retains campaign data for 12 months after the promotion closes.
  10. This promotion is conducted under the Gambling Act, 2012 with the approval of the Gambling Authority of Botswana.
